About Malabar Bites

Malabar Bites brings the authentic taste of Kerala and South Indian cuisine to Coventry. Our chefs prepare traditional dishes using fresh ingredients and authentic spices inspired by the coastal region of Kerala.


From flavourful Malabar biryanis and crispy dosas to freshly made parottas and rich curries, every meal is crafted to deliver a genuine culinary experience.
